Findings & decisions

What this fixes

  • The percentage is gone — the count is countable, and the caption sits outside the pill, so the <30% low-fill label guard retires with it.
  • Splits explain themselves — a split is a visibly divided block, which is why the tally says 16½.
  • Every candidate appears, not just the leader; the full field breakdown is the meter.
  • Inspectable — each block carries its endorsement on hover/focus.
  • Non-majority races work — no label inside the fill, so nothing breaks under 50%.

Decided · Aug 4, 2026

  • Denominator. An explicit “no endorsement” is the same non-signal as no coverage — the site is about endorsement weight, not sources. Declining sources get no block and no weight; fill-length keeps meaning share of endorsements.
  • Block width may vary by race. Meter-to-meter comparability beats block-to-block visual weight; the goal is helping a voter decide, not preserving block size across races.
  • Split placement optimizes visual continuity, not pixel precision.
  • Inspection begets discovery. On desktop the resting meter shows the familiar left-aligned percent on a solid fill — at first glance the only change is softer corners. Hover trades the percent for hairline seams — darker than the fill, just barely showing the segmentation — and per-block tooltips. On touch there is no percent: seams are always visible and tapping a block is the discovery path.
  • Color commitments are exactly three: majority = the site’s own teal #087f73, tie = variations of amber (a sole no-majority leader keeps the current meter’s amber), selected candidate = bold. Trailing candidates wear the muted set (slate / taupe / plum) — distinguishable, yet obviously less relevant. No other hue carries meaning.
